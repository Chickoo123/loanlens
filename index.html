Check a loan offer against RBI rules before you sign.

Copy the figures from your loan's Key Facts Statement (KFS), which RBI requires for every retail loan. LoanLens recomputes the true cost, checks the offer against specific RBI rules, and tells you what to raise with the lender.

  • RBI's APR method. It reproduces the worked example in RBI's KFS circular (17.07%).
  • Every finding is cited. Each one links to the RBI circular it relies on.
  • Private by design. It runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent or stored.

How LoanLens calculates

  1. Net amount received = sanctioned amount − processing fee (plus 18% GST unless already included) − insurance − other upfront charges − broken-period interest − instalments collected upfront.
  2. APR = the monthly rate that makes the remaining EMIs worth exactly the net amount received, × 12. This is a plain internal rate of return on a reducing balance, matching the illustration in Annex B of RBI's KFS circular. LoanLens also shows the APR without GST, because lenders differ on whether GST is included. A difference of 0.25 points or less is treated as a match.
  3. Rule checks compare your figures with the specific practices RBI has prohibited. Each finding shows the arithmetic, the rule, and what to ask the lender.

What LoanLens cannot tell you

  • Whether a lender is registered with RBI. Check the lender on RBI's website before borrowing.
  • Anything that isn't in the figures you enter. If the KFS is wrong or missing, the result can be too.
  • Legal advice. Findings are prompts to ask the lender, not determinations. Rules differ by lender type, and RBI updates them.
